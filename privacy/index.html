適用範圍與聯絡方式

本政策由 NickHuang Studio 提供，適用於「黃泉路」（YOMIAME，部分版本名稱為「黃泉雨」）遊戲及其封閉測試。Android 套件名稱為 com.kage.yomiame。本政策說明目前版本的資料處理方式。

如有隱私問題，或要要求查詢、更正、刪除您提供的測試資料，請寄信至 warhammerlieutenant@gmail.com。

遊戲本身的資料處理

目前遊戲不需要註冊或登入，不設玩家帳號，不向開發者伺服器傳送個人資料，也不內建廣告、廣告追蹤、分析服務或第三方崩潰回報 SDK。我們不透過遊戲收集、分享或販售您的個人資料。

遊戲不要求存取您的聯絡人、精確位置、相機或麥克風，也不讀取廣告識別碼。遊戲會在裝置內儲存音量、音樂音量與畫質設定，以便下次啟動時沿用。這些設定不會傳送給開發者。目前挑戰進度僅保留在執行中的記憶體，結束應用程式後即清除。

封閉測試與主動提供的回饋

若您或您授權的測試安排者提供 Google 帳號 Email，我們會將該地址加入 Google Play Console 的測試名單，讓您取得測試資格。名單不會公開在本網站。加入名單後，仍需由您自行在 Google Play 同意加入測試。

如果您主動寄送回饋，我們會收到您的寄件地址、訊息內容，以及您自願附上的裝置型號、系統版本、截圖或其他附件。我們僅為測試管理、回覆問題及修正遊戲處理這些資料。請不要提供密碼、付款資訊或與遊戲問題無關的敏感資料。

服務提供者

Google Play 負責應用程式配送、測試資格及平台自身的診斷與統計處理；Google 與電子郵件服務商會依各自政策處理您使用其服務時提供的資料。這不代表遊戲本身內建資料蒐集 SDK。請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。

本網站以 GitHub Pages 託管。瀏覽網站時，GitHub 可能處理 IP 位址及連線紀錄以提供與保護服務，詳見 GitHub 隱私權聲明。本網站不加入分析碼、廣告追蹤或自行設定追蹤 Cookie。

保留、安全與刪除

裝置內設定由您控制，可透過 Android 的清除應用程式資料功能或解除安裝刪除。由於遊戲沒有玩家帳號，也沒有開發者持有的雲端遊戲存檔，因此無須另外申請刪除遊戲帳號。

測試名單與回饋只保留於測試管理、問題處理及必要紀錄所需期間，不再需要時刪除。我們將存取限於處理這些工作的授權人員，並使用服務提供者的存取控制。您可以透過上述信箱要求刪除我們持有的測試地址與回饋；如依法必須保留部分資料，我們會說明原因。Google 或 GitHub 自行持有的資料，須依其程序提出請求。

兒童與政策更新

遊戲沒有個人資料輸入欄位，也不為兒童建立個人資料檔案。若您認為兒童透過測試回饋向我們提供了個人資料，請聯絡我們處理刪除。

新增功能或資料處理方式改變時，我們會更新本政策與頁面日期，並在適用情況下更新 Google Play 資料安全性聲明及提供必要通知。

Scope and contact

This policy is provided by NickHuang Studio for the game 黃泉路 (YOMIAME, called 黃泉雨 in some versions) and its closed testing program. The Android package name is com.kage.yomiame. This policy describes the current version.

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete testing information you provided, contact warhammerlieutenant@gmail.com.

Data handled by the game

The current game does not require registration or sign-in, has no player accounts, and does not transmit personal data to a developer server. It includes no advertising, advertising tracking, analytics services or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect, share or sell personal data through the game.

The game does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera or microphone, and does not read advertising identifiers. Audio volume, music volume and graphics settings are saved locally on your device for future sessions and are not sent to the developer. Current challenge progress is held only in running memory and is cleared when the application ends.

Closed testing and feedback you provide

If you or a testing coordinator authorized by you supplies your Google Account email address, we add it to a tester list in Google Play Console to provide testing eligibility. The list is not published on this website. You must separately opt in to testing on Google Play.

If you voluntarily email feedback, we receive your sender address, message and any device model, operating system version, screenshots or other attachments you choose to include. We use this information only to administer testing, respond and fix the game. Do not send passwords, payment information or sensitive information unrelated to the issue.

Service providers

Google Play handles distribution, testing eligibility and its own platform diagnostics and statistics. Google and email providers process information supplied through their services under their own policies. This does not mean that the game embeds a data-collection SDK. See the Google Privacy Policy.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and connection logs to operate and protect its services, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website adds no analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies of its own.

Retention, security and deletion

You control local settings and can remove them by clearing app data in Android or uninstalling the app. There are no player accounts or developer-held cloud saves, so no separate game account deletion request is needed.

We retain tester lists and feedback only for as long as needed for testing administration, issue resolution and necessary records, then delete them. Access is limited to authorized people performing these tasks using the service providers' access controls. You can request deletion of testing addresses and feedback we hold using the contact above. If any information must be retained by law, we will explain why. Requests concerning information held independently by Google or GitHub must follow their procedures.

Children and policy updates

The game has no personal-data entry fields and does not create personal profiles of children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information through testing feedback so we can address deletion.

When features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its date, update the Google Play Data safety declaration where applicable, and provide any required notice.
